    How it decides

    Each payment collects points from the checks below. Some checks compare a payment with the rest of your file (for example, the median amount), so the result is relative to your business, not a global rule.

    • Fraud: 60 points or more, or the status already shows a dispute, return or reversal.
    • Review: 30 to 59 points. Worth a quick verification.
    • Legit: under 30 points, with enough information to judge.
    • Not enough information: the amount or date is missing, or fewer than 4 checks could run on that row.

    This is a proof of concept. It points out payments worth a second look; it can't prove fraud or innocence, and the weights are starting points, not tuned on real data. Use it alongside your payment provider's own tools, not instead of them.
